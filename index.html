Personal Automation Assistant

This is a private personal automation application used by its owner to integrate Google services with personal productivity and automation workflows.

Powered by OpenClaw, the application is for its owner's personal use. It is not a public SaaS product and is not a service sold or offered to other users.

Privacy Policy

Google services

Depending on the workflow and permissions granted, the application may access:

  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Drive
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets
  • Google Contacts

Authorization and data use

The Google Account owner must explicitly authorize access through OAuth before the application can access Google user data. Access is limited to the permissions the account owner grants.

Google user data is used only to carry out automation requested by the user, such as organizing information or managing personal productivity tasks. Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

This public information website requires no login and uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking.
